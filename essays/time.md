 ## Prelude… 
 
I spend many nights late, agonizing nights contemplating my life decisions. I’d wake up at 3 in the morning feeling the tension in my jaw. With my eyes heavy and my body felt like the air gained 20 pounds. I’d spend hours tossing and turning,  asking myself  “what should I do…. Can I do that?!” 

Only to wake up in my junior year, realizing years of nail biting and teeth grinding had finally eroded my teeth. The years of sleepless nights and waking up at 4 a.m. made my eyes hollow and sullen. In other words, how I was spending my time was catching up to me. I was a nervous mess. So terrified to make a wrong decision, I usually never made one.  

## Context… 
A little bit about me. I was illiterate as a child. I struggled in my formative years and was relegated to special ed classes due to a learning disability. Kids called me dumb… despite the progression of my grades. In high school, a family member told me “college isn’t really for you, you're a bit slow.” All the while, I maintained an honor roll status. I would be lying if I said it didn’t bother me, that I didn’t carry their words around, or though they were right on certain nights . 

## Then came a realization…
So at this point, why am I telling you this?? Why am I dumping all my problems on the unsuspecting, passing reader? Well, I wanted to share something that saved me much headache and heartache. Time… is your number one non-renewable resource. It is fleeting and finite! And you’ll never get it back. Thinking of all the mental gymnastics and ruminating you’ve done in your lifetime. The hours or days lamenting about something. All for you to usually go with your initial, gut instinct.

## Wrap it up… 
In programming ideas like efficiency and refinement are prized. Your growth comes from the project you do and the mistakes you make along the way. That being said, 
What can be gained by never taking the first steps?  What mistakes can be made if you never even start? 

What I'm trying to get at is… imperfect action will always beat perfect inaction. The amount of time you have spent thinking about the thing, you probably could have done it once or twice over! And what is efficient about that?  Indecision will rob you of your time, opportunities, and learning. This isn’t a cosign from me to be reckless. Or to take home that questionable person from the bar. But reclaim your time! Take back your headspace. Use it towards forward moving action and personal betterment.
