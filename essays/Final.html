Problem

Every student, at some point, has pulled an all-nighter, crammed for an exam, or struggled to review messy notes. Not to mention the challenge of scheduling classes, fulfilling graduation requirements, and accessing required materials. Moreover, many students have taken a class that blindsided them with unexpected expectations, required readings, and material not covered in prerequisite courses.

Solution

Some students ask a friend, others go in blind, or they can access the easy-to-use PrepHub. A hosting platform provides UH's resource directory that prepare students for the rigor of their courses and alleviates academic stress. It is comprised of a database that allows students to collaborate, review, and access materials needed for their courses at UH Mānoa.

landing page

  • Listed directories

Curriculum

Connect

  • Ask for tutors
  • Collaborative study groups
Account
  • Signing in
  • Profile
  • Selecting students or contributors

Use case ideas:

  • Searching for a class you plan to take for an upcoming semester
  • Looking up graduation requirements or dual graduate attributes
  • Finding the latest class format and grading distribution
  • Comparing or searching up notes from a class lecture
  • Requesting scribes or tutoring for a class
  • Connecting to classmates or existing study groups

Beyond the basics:

  • You can access other UH students that have your same major, making communication and access to study groups easier.
  • Set a profile pictures
  • Add a listing of all completed courses with respect to their major requirements (ex: ICS 111, ICS 211, ICS 212, etc.), helping others to reach out for help or see posted old notes from completed courses.
  • Request services portal for people offering tutoring or TAs
