So if you have read any of my other posts, I’ve spent my fair share of writing being on my soap box. Force feeding my own ideals to the passing reader and exposing facebook style motivation content. In this segment I want to take a step back from contemplations of morals and ethics. Let's instead shift focus, and let me tell you a bit about myself.


## A little background… 


I am from Haiku, the north shore of Maui. It was an amazing place to grow up! As a kid, I spent my free time dirt biking in cane fields and surfing at my homebreak, Ho’okipa.

However, my family and I moved from Maui to O’ahu when I was 9 years old. The recession hit your community hard. Moving to the capital of Hawai’i was a decision that would allow my parents better work opportunities. As well as provide me with a better education.  Over the years, I’ve contemplated moving back, but could bring myself to do so. For one, my family is all here. Secondly, Oahu’s north shore is the mecca of surfing. How could I ever pass those up? 


## When passion meets projects… 

As for my interest in computer science, I have a fascination with design and applications. Growing up I have always loved art. Whether that be portraits, pastels, or paintings. Anything to capture my gaze longer than my short lived attention span. 

That being said, I think eye-catching visuals combined with pertinent information is very powerful.  For this reason, I have been learning data visualization and graphics for my work as a data energy analyst on campus. My job has taught me how to communicate massive amounts of data and how to package it to the viewers in a digestible way. 

Being new, I have much to learn! However, I look forward to developing my software programming skills in tandem with my work at UH Mānoa. Ultimately, with the hopes of providing accessible information and resources to my peers and future students to come.
